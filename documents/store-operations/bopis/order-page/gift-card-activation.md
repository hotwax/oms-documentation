# Gift Card Activation

The BOPIS App allows store staff to activate gift cards directly while fulfilling orders. This helps in handling gift card items without switching to any other system.

## When Does the Gift Card Activation popup Appear?

The activation pop-up opens when a staff member taps on a gift card item from the orders list.

## Steps to Activate a Gift Card

**Step 1: Enter the Activation Code**  
The staff member can scan or manually type the serial number printed on the physical gift card. It’s important to make sure the code is correct before moving forward.

**Step 2: Tap the Activate Button**  
After entering the code, tapping the Activate button (represented by a gift card icon in the corner) will bring up a confirmation message. This is the final opportunity to review the entered details.

**Step 3: Confirm Activation**  
Tapping Activate again on the confirmation popup completes the process. If activation is successful, a confirmation message appears, and the gift card number is displayed along with the activation timestamp.

## Important Notes

- Once a gift card is activated, the details can’t be changed.  
- If activation doesn’t go through, an error message will appear. It helps to recheck the code and try again.  
- Some stores may notify customers after activation, so it’s good practice to review everything carefully before confirming.

## Product Information Display

To help verify the gift card item, the app displays the product name and price, other identifiers like SKU or barcode may also be shown.
