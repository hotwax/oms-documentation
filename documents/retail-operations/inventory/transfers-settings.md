# Transfers App Settings

Use the `Settings` tab to review your profile, switch product store context, and update app preferences.

## Profile actions
The profile card shows your user ID and name.

From this card, you can:

- `Logout`
- `Go to Launchpad`

## OMS information
The `OMS` section shows the OMS instance you are currently connected to.

- Review the current instance before you continue.
- Select `Go to OMS` to open the connected OMS environment.

## Product store
Use the product store selector to switch the current store context for the app.

Changing the product store also refreshes store-specific facilities and product identifier preferences.

## Product identifier
Use `Product Identifier` to choose the identifiers you want to display in product search results and item lists.

## Timezone
Use the timezone control to update the timezone used in the app.
