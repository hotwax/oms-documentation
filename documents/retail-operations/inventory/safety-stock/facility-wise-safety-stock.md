# Set facility-wise safety stock

Use a safety stock rule in the **Order Routing App** when the same inventory reserve should apply to a facility group.

1. Identify or create the facility groups that need a shared safety stock value. See [Manage facility groups](/documents/system-admin/administration/facilities/manage-groups.md).
2. Open the **Order Routing App**, then go to `Safety stock`.
3. Create a rule and enter its `Name` and `Safety stock` value.
4. Select all facility groups or add groups to `Included` and `Excluded`.
5. Optional: Narrow the rule with product tags and features.
6. Save the rule, then run or schedule the safety stock computation.

See [Configure safety stock rules](../../orders/order-routing/sourcing/safety-stock.md) for complete field, sequence, and schedule guidance.
