# Schedule sourcing rules

Threshold, safety stock, store pickup, and shipping rules are computed by separate rule groups. Each rule page has a `Schedule` card when active rules exist.

## Activate a recurring schedule

1. Open the rule page in the **Order Routing App**.
2. Review the `Run time` shown on the `Schedule` card.
3. Select `Schedule` to activate the displayed recurring schedule.

The card shows the next run when the schedule is active. A paused schedule shows `Paused` and no next run time.

## Run rules immediately

Use `Run now` after creating or changing rules when you do not want to wait for the recurring run.

1. Open the overflow menu on the `Schedule` card.
2. Select `Run now`.
3. Confirm the action.

Running rules now creates an immediate copy of the scheduled job. It does not replace the recurring schedule.

## Review or disable a schedule

Open the schedule overflow menu and select:

* `History` to review previous rule-group runs.
* `Disable` to pause future recurring runs.

Disabling a schedule does not delete its sourcing rules.
