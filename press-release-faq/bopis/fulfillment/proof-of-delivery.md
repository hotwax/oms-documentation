# Press Release

## Proof of Delivery for BOPIS Orders

Record explicit confirmation when a BOPIS order is handed over to the customer, creating a reliable source of truth for pickup completion.

**Subheading:** For store teams and retail operations leaders who need clear accountability and auditability at the moment a BOPIS order is picked up.

## Summary

Proof of Delivery introduces an explicit confirmation step during BOPIS handover to record that an order was successfully delivered to the customer. Instead of relying solely on order status progression, stores can now capture delivery confirmation at the exact moment of pickup.

This creates a clear, consistent record of handover, helping retailers reduce disputes, standardize store behavior, and strengthen trust in BOPIS completion data.

## Problem

BOPIS orders are often marked completed based on system workflow rather than a verified handover event. When customers later claim an order was not received, store teams frequently lack concrete evidence to confirm what happened at pickup.

As a result, operations teams spend time investigating disputes, reviewing logs, or manually reconciling records, often with inconsistent outcomes across stores.

This gap becomes more pronounced in high-volume pickup environments, where multiple associates handle handovers and accountability is shared. Without a clear proof of delivery, even correctly completed pickups can appear ambiguous after the fact.

## Solution

Proof of Delivery adds a structured confirmation step to the BOPIS handover process. When an order is handed to the customer, store associates explicitly confirm delivery as part of the pickup flow. This confirmation is captured alongside the completed order, ensuring that the final order state reflects an actual handover event rather than an assumed one.

By making delivery confirmation a part of pickup, Proof of Delivery standardizes how stores complete BOPIS orders and creates a dependable record that can be referenced when questions arise.

## Call to Action

Use Proof of Delivery during BOPIS handover to ensure every completed pickup has a clear confirmation record.  
Rely on this visibility to reduce customer disputes, simplify investigations, and improve confidence in BOPIS completion across stores.
