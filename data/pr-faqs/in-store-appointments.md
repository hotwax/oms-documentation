# Press Release

## In-Store Appointments

Deliver premium in-store appointment booking experiences that drive foot traffic, increase conversion rates, and build lasting customer relationships.

**Subheading:** For retail operations and store teams who want to offer personalized VIP experiences and attract high-intent buyers to their stores.


## Summary

In-Store Appointments allow customers to book personalized shopping experiences directly from a retailer's website or mobile app. By capturing customer preferences and styling needs beforehand, store associates can curate selections and prepare fitting rooms prior to arrival. This solution helps retailers reduce no-shows through automated reminders, optimize staff allocation, and maximize sales opportunities by providing dedicated, expert service.


## Problem

- High-intent customers often lack a convenient way to schedule dedicated, personalized time with in-store experts.
- Store associates miss out on upselling opportunities when they are unprepared for a customer's specific needs, sizes, or preferences.
- Unpredictable foot traffic and appointment no-shows make it difficult for store managers to efficiently allocate resources and staff.


## Solution

- A customizable online booking widget lets customers schedule appointments and fill out preparation questionnaires with their preferences.
- Store associates receive push notifications and can access customer context via a mobile-first dashboard to pre-select items and prepare for the visit.
- Automated email and SMS reminders keep customers informed, significantly reducing no-shows while keeping the store team organized.


## Getting Started

In-Store Appointments are managed directly through the Store App and customer-facing booking widget. Customers select an appointment type (e.g., styling, fitting), choose a store, and complete a brief questionnaire. Store teams receive the request, prepare the tailored experience, and if an item is unavailable during the visit, they can use Endless Aisle capabilities to ship the desired product directly to the customer.


## Call to Action

- Implement the online booking widget to start attracting high-intent buyers and offering white-glove VIP service.
- Equip your store teams with the Store App to manage appointments efficiently and prepare personalized curations for every visit.
