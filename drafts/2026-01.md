# January 2026 Release Notes

January brings updates that help you track inventory movements and manage fulfillment across your stores. These changes focus on better reconciliation with external platforms and more control over how store teams handle pickups and transfers.

## Store Operations
### Transfer Orders
We updated Transfer Order management to make sure inventory remains accurate when moving stock between locations. The `Receiving App` now sends push notifications for new orders so your team can act quickly. Mis-shipped items are now tracked through the entire receiving process and show up in completed views. We also updated the NetSuite connector to reconcile shipments and receipts more accurately, including support for completed orders and mis-shipped items.

## Inventory Management
### Inventory Tracking and Counting
Users now only see inventory logs for the facilities they are assigned to. We updated the calculations for weighted average cost and quantity on hand to improve data accuracy. The `Inventory Count App` now includes live search, confirmation modals for removing scans, and the option to clear local data. Additionally, the system now filters out discontinued products during searches and supports primary and secondary ID lookups for reviewing stock variances.
