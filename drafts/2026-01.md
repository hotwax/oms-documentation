# 2026-01 Release Notes

Happy New Year! We’re kicking off 2026 with a focus on enhancing your BOPIS experience and improving overall system performance. This release delivers key improvements to order handover, proof of delivery, and fulfillment flexibility, all designed to make your operations smoother and your customers happier.

## BOPIS App
### Proof of Delivery Enhancements
We’ve launched Proof of Delivery to enhance order fulfillment and customer confidence! This feature allows store associates to capture customer confirmation – including proof of identity – at the time of pickup directly within the OMS. New settings and permissions provide you with granular control, and customers will automatically receive an email notification confirming their order has been successfully collected.

### Order Handover & Notifications
We’ve streamlined the Buy Online, Pick Up In Store (BOPIS) experience with automated handover emails that notify customers when their entire order is ready. You’ll also see improved in-app notifications confirming order handover and arrival, providing clearer feedback to your team. These enhancements ensure customers receive timely updates and a polished experience, even with split orders – only sending the completion email when everything is ready for pickup.

### UI/UX Improvements
We’ve refined the loading experience to provide clearer guidance while pages are processing. Previously, the loading message was somewhat ambiguous; now, you’ll see more helpful text like “Loading… Please wait” or “Processing your request…” to keep shoppers informed. This improvement ensures a smoother, more intuitive experience during key interactions within the BOPIS app.

### SearchBar Static
You’ll now see the search bar remain visible as you scroll within the Assign Picker Modal. We’ve updated the search bar to a static position for improved usability during item assignment. This enhancement streamlines the picking process and reduces unnecessary scrolling, ultimately saving you time.

## Order Management
### BOPIS to ShipToStore Implementation
You can now seamlessly transition Buy Online, Pick Up In Store (BOPIS) orders to Ship-to-Store fulfillment. This enhancement allows you to leverage store inventory for online orders, improving fulfillment flexibility and speed. We’ve refined the workflow to ensure accurate order status updates and a smooth experience for both your team and customers, including improved order filtering and notifications.

### Order Picker Flow Improvements
We’ve enhanced the order picker flow to maintain data integrity and streamline operations. The system now prevents edits to picker assignments once an order is handed over to the customer or if the order is rejected. This ensures accurate order fulfillment records and a smoother experience for your team.

## Settings & Configuration
### Shipping Orders Description Update
The “Shipping Orders” setting description was unclear, potentially causing confusion for users. We clarified the description to “View only shipping orders in the application” and added translations for Spanish and Japanese. This update ensures a more intuitive and globally accessible experience when configuring order visibility within your HotWax Commerce system.

## System & Core Updates
### Code Cleanup & Technical Debt Reduction
We’ve streamlined the system by removing unused code and configurations, resulting in a cleaner and more efficient application. This includes updating environment settings for product store enums and eliminating obsolete references to Solr. You’ll experience improved performance and a more maintainable system with these updates.
