# HotWax Commerce Product Update

This month, we’ve focused on expanding fulfillment options, enhancing inventory accuracy, and making the store associate experience more intuitive across our suite of apps.

---

## 🚀 New Features

### Ship to Store fulfillment
HotWax Commerce now supports "Ship to Store" workflows within the BOPIS App. 
*   **User Benefit:** You can now offer customers the flexibility to have products shipped from a distribution center to a specific store for pickup, broadening your omnichannel fulfillment options.

### Order handover notifications
We’ve added automated email support for order handovers and enhanced status notifications.
*   **User Benefit:** Keep your customers informed with real-time updates when their orders are ready for pickup or have been successfully handed over.

### Proof of delivery for pickups
A new proof of delivery setting is now available in the BOPIS App, complete with specific user permissions.
*   **User Benefit:** Enhance security and order accuracy by requiring a verification step during the customer pickup process.

### Real-time alerts for transfer orders
The Receiving App now supports push notifications for new transfer orders.
*   **User Benefit:** Receiving teams stay informed with instant alerts on their devices when new stock is on its way, allowing for better labor planning.

---

## ⚡ Improvements

### Enhanced product identification in inventory counts
We added support for primary and secondary identifiers (such as internal names or SKUs) across the Assigned, Pending Review, and Closed lists in the Inventory Count App.
*   **User Benefit:** Find and sort products more easily during counts using the identifiers that make the most sense for your warehouse or store.

### Partial sync for inventory records
The Inventory Count App now handles missing "Quantity on Hand" (QOH) data more intelligently. If an error occurs with one record during a sync, the app will block only that record and allow the rest of the count to sync successfully.
*   **User Benefit:** Prevent your entire inventory sync from getting stuck due to a single data error, ensuring most of your work is saved immediately.

### Local database management
Users can now clear cached data directly from the `Settings` menu in the Inventory Count App.
*   **User Benefit:** Quickly troubleshoot local data issues or refresh your app's cache without needing technical assistance.

### Improved tracking for mis-shipped items
Mis-shipped items are now visible in both the `All` and `Completed` tabs of the Receiving App.
*   **User Benefit:** Gain better visibility into shipment discrepancies and maintain more accurate receiving records.

### Static search bars
We’ve updated the search bars in the BOPIS and Fulfillment Apps to remain static at the top of the screen.
*   **User Benefit:** Save time by having search tools always accessible, even when scrolling through long lists of orders.

### Optimized inventory filters
Added a filter to exclude "Discontinued" category products during inventory counts.
*   **User Benefit:** Keep your staff focused on active inventory by removing discontinued items from active count lists.

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

*   **Facility details:** Fixed an issue in the `Facility Address Modal` where the `Save` button remained disabled when removing a facility phone number. You can now update or remove contact details without errors.
*   **Accurate scan removal:** Fixed a bug in the Inventory Count App that allowed users to remove the same scan event multiple times. A confirmation modal has been added to ensure quantities remain accurate.
*   **Out-of-stock adjustments:** Fixed a synchronization issue where system inventory levels were not updating correctly when marking uncounted items as "out of stock."
*   **Picker assignments:** Corrected a workflow in the BOPIS App that previously allowed users to edit assigned pickers even after an order was rejected.
*   **Carrier selection:** Restricted carrier selection for "Ship to Store" orders in the Fulfillment App to ensure only valid shipping methods are used.
*   **Display fixes:** Resolved an issue where the logout loader would persist unnecessarily and fixed a bug in the Receiving App that occasionally caused products to be invisible.
